j · ge · gi

Como una h inglesa fuerte · Like a strong English h

Tema · Topic: 👪 Las personas · People

How to say it

The back of your tongue goes up, very close to the back of the roof of your mouth (the velo). The air passes through the small space and makes friction: jjjj.

It's like the Scottish loch, or a cat's hhh! 🐱

Ojo · Careful
ge and gi sound like j: gente, girasol. But ga, go, gu are like go.
