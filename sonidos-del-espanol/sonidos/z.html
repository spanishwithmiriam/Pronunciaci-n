ce · ci · z

«th» en España · «s» en Latinoamérica · «th» in Spain · «s» in Latin America

Tema · Topic: 🏙️ La ciudad · The city

How to say it

In Spain, z and c + e, i sound like the th in think: the tip of your tongue goes between your teeth.

In Latin America (and the Canary Islands) they sound like s. Both are correct!

Ojo · Careful
Spelling: za, zo, zu use z; ce, ci use c. ciudad = "thiu-dad" in Spain.
